h3-kaggle-studio

First run

Render video on a free GPU

This studio submits your shots to Kaggle, which runs the MiniMax H3 video model on a free GPU. Each run takes about 2 hours. Up to 6 shots per run, 6 to 15 seconds each. Your videos are stored on this machine only.

  • Up to 6 shotsone kernel, one queue slot
  • 6–15 secondsper shot, per render
  • ~2 hourswall time for a full run

You will connect your own Kaggle account. This studio has no account of its own and stores nothing on a server of ours.

Rules

Kaggle's rules

Renders run on your own Kaggle account and your own weekly GPU quota (30 hours, resetting weekly).

Kaggle's Terms of Use limit free compute to “your own internal, personal, non-commercial use, and not on behalf of or for the benefit of any third party”. Rendering paid client work on Kaggle's free GPU breaks that term, and Kaggle's stated consequence is termination of your account. If you are rendering for clients, use a paid GPU or ask Kaggle for written permission.

Kaggle also requires phone verification before it will give you a GPU, and allows one account per person. Do not share your account, do not resell access to it, and do not run this studio as a backend for other people's requests: Kaggle treats that as server farming and closes accounts for it.

Step 1 of 2

Get your Kaggle token

  1. Sign in or create an account: kaggle.com/account/login
  2. Verify your phone number if you have not already: kaggle.com/settings
  3. Open kaggle.com/settings/api and select “Generate New Token” under API.
  4. Copy the whole token. It is one long string; the page only shows it once.

If you already have a kaggle.json file on this machine, you can drop it in on the next pane instead.

Step 2 of 2

Connect your account

Paste the token, or drop your kaggle.json here. The token is written to ~/.kaggle/access_token with owner-only permissions and is never shown again, not even to you.
